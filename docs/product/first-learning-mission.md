# First Learning Mission

## Status

Approved first-mission baseline.

- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-29

## Purpose

Validate the complete LingoCoon learning loop with one short, realistic mission. The mission must demonstrate personalization and all four core skills before the product expands in breadth.

## Recommended learner and scenario

- Learner: adult beginner or early-intermediate learner.
- Target language: English.
- Situation: introduce yourself in a real work context.
- Session length: 5–10 minutes.
- Speaking turns: 3–5.
- Primary outcome: deliver a short, understandable self-introduction and answer one follow-up question without reading a model sentence.

## Personalization inputs

- learner's native language and target language;
- self-assessed level;
- work-related goal;
- selected interests or professional context;
- known vocabulary and recent errors;
- preferred feedback language and intensity;
- available session time.

If evidence is missing, the mission uses a conservative default and asks the learner to adjust it.

## Mission flow

### 1. Orientation

The raccoon introduces the situation, outcome, estimated duration, and optional difficulty adjustment.

### 2. Context reading

The learner reads a short dialogue between two people meeting at work. Important phrases can be opened for meaning, pronunciation, or example usage.

Completion evidence includes reading completion and optional help requests.

### 3. Listening

The learner hears the dialogue once at natural speed and once at a slower speed. A small number of comprehension questions check meaning rather than word-by-word recall.

### 4. Guided writing

The learner writes a short self-introduction using optional prompts. The system identifies the highest-value correction and explains it in the learner's preferred feedback language.

### 5. Speaking

The AI asks the learner to introduce themselves by voice and then asks one follow-up question. The learner can request a hint, replay the prompt, or retry.

The first evaluation separates:

- task completion;
- comprehensibility;
- target vocabulary use;
- one priority language correction;
- confidence of the evaluation.

### 6. Retry

The learner repeats the task with a changed prompt or new detail. The product highlights improvement rather than presenting a full error inventory.

### 7. Review

The learner receives:

- one strength;
- one priority improvement;
- useful vocabulary or grammar to revisit;
- a short explanation of the next recommended activity;
- controls to save, dismiss, or edit the recommendation.

## Learning events

The mission should record structured events for reading, listening, writing, speaking, hint use, retry, evaluation confidence, and recommendation outcome. Raw audio is not retained by default.

## Acceptance criteria

- A new learner can understand the objective without external explanation.
- The activity can be completed using keyboard and touch.
- Reading, listening, writing, and speaking all contribute to the same mission outcome.
- The learner can finish a speaking attempt without being forced to accept every correction.
- At least one useful signal is saved for later personalization.
- The next recommendation is explainable and can be overridden.
- AI, speech, or audio failure produces a usable fallback.
- The mission does not reward repeating a memorized sentence without responding to the task.

The v1 scenario is a workplace introduction. Additional scenarios require evidence from the first vertical slice and a recorded product decision.
