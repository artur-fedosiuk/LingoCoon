# LingoCoon Product Vision

## Status

Approved product foundation.

- Version: 1.0
- Last reviewed: 2026-08-29

## Vision

LingoCoon helps people become capable of using a language in real situations. It adapts practice to the learner instead of forcing every learner through the same sequence.

## Product promise

> LingoCoon understands what the learner needs next and creates a useful way to practice it.

## Product model

LingoCoon combines two paths:

- **Guided path:** the app recommends the next activity from goals, performance, errors, interests, and available time.
- **Free path:** the learner requests a topic, situation, explanation, correction, or practice format and remains in control.

Both paths update the same learner profile when the learner permits it.

## Learning principles

1. Practice starts in context and moves toward independent production.
2. Reading, writing, listening, and speaking are connected rather than isolated modes.
3. Speaking is a primary capability and must be safe to attempt frequently.
4. Feedback is selective, understandable, and followed by a chance to retry.
5. Repeated evidence matters more than one mistake.
6. AI evaluations express uncertainty and do not overcorrect acceptable language.
7. Personalization changes the activity, difficulty, content, and review timing.
8. Gamification rewards useful learning behavior rather than empty interaction.
9. The raccoon guides and encourages the learner without competing with the learning task.
10. The system collects only data with a defined learning purpose and retention rule.

## Experience principles

- Always show the learner what to do next and why it was chosen.
- Make the first meaningful action possible within seconds.
- Let the learner change the topic, difficulty, skill, or activity type without losing progress.
- Keep sessions short enough to finish and substantial enough to produce evidence.
- Prefer one clear correction over an overwhelming list of corrections.
- Design for keyboard, touch, assistive technology, slow networks, and unavailable AI services.

## Success definition

The product is successful when learners can produce more useful language independently over time, not merely when they complete more drills or maintain a longer streak.

## Non-goals for the foundation

- Copying another product's mascot, illustrations, or interaction patterns.
- Treating flashcards as the primary learning experience.
- Building live multiplayer before the individual learning loop is effective.
- Adding unrestricted AI autonomy without contracts, evaluation, and cost controls.
