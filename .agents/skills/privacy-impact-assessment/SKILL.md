# Privacy Impact Assessment

## Procedure

1. Define purpose, users, data categories, sources, necessity, and whether minors or special-category data may be involved.
2. Map collection, browser/server/database storage, provider recipients, geographic transfer, access, logs, analytics, and derived inference.
3. Define consent or legal-basis questions, transparency, retention, correction, export, deletion, objections, and processor evidence.
4. Minimize raw audio, transcripts, prompts, personal identifiers, and retention; keep model inference distinct from user-provided facts.
5. Assess re-identification, breach, overcollection, inaccurate inference, dark patterns, and AI Act applicability questions.
6. Identify engineering controls, operator actions, provider verification, and professional legal review triggers.

Return a processing table, necessity decision, risks, controls, retention/deletion contract, user-right flows, and blockers. Do not approve processing or modify policies/external services without explicit authority.
