# LingoCoon Research

Produce concise, decision-ready research for LingoCoon. Use this skill for competitor analysis, learning-method research, technology evaluation, source verification, or repository-informed product discovery.

This skill never expands the user's authorization to edit the repository or an external system.

## Workflow

1. State the exact question and decision it should inform.
2. Inspect the relevant repository files before making implementation assumptions.
3. Prefer primary sources: official product documentation, research papers, official announcements, and first-party repositories.
4. Verify current or changing claims at research time and record publication or access dates.
5. Separate observed facts, interpretation, recommendation, and uncertainty.
6. Compare alternatives against learner value, feasibility, accessibility, privacy, performance, and zero-budget constraints.
7. Stop when the decision is supported; do not expand into unrelated research.

For competitor interaction work, inspect the actual flow when accessible, or use the supplied screenshots with explicit limits. Official feature documentation verifies a documented capability, not hands-on usability or learning efficacy. Label evidence as observed, documented, or inferred. Finish with the few concrete interaction/acceptance changes supported by the evidence; do not repeat a broad market landscape when the implementation decision is already clear.

## Required output

- question and scope;
- sources with direct links and authority level;
- evidence matrix with claim, source, date, and confidence;
- implications for LingoCoon;
- recommendation and realistic alternatives;
- open questions and next decision.

Do not present search snippets, marketing claims, or community examples as verified facts. Do not modify code, documents, services, or external systems unless separately authorized.
